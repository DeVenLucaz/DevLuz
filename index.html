THE BUILDER

Who is DeVen?

A vibe-coder. Not a developer in any traditional sense — no CS degree, no PC, no IDE, no desk setup. I build everything on an Android phone using Termux. I've been writing fiction for 9+ years. I've been building software for about 6 months.

The vibe comes first, the architecture follows — but the architecture is actually good. I orchestrate AI models to execute my vision. Claude handles architecture and planning. Antigravity CLI handles implementation. I handle everything else.

I build things that may not be available to most — built exactly to my own convenience and specifications.
