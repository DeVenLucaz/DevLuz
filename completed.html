llamdrop 🦙

Run AI on any device. No PC. No subscription. No struggle.

llamdrop is a free, open-source tool that lets anyone run a local AI model on whatever device they own — an Android phone via Termux, an old laptop, a Raspberry Pi, or a budget PC. It reads your hardware automatically, detects your exact chip, RAM, and GPU, finds AI models that fit your specs, downloads them, and runs them.

Key Features (v0.10.0)

  • Device Intelligence: Full device profiling with a 5-tier classification (Micro to High).
  • Dynamic Backend Probing: Runs a 10-second micro-benchmark on first boot to guarantee the fastest and most stable backend (CPU vs GPU).
  • Model Browser: Verified catalog curated for your specific hardware tier, plus live HuggingFace search.
  • Auto-Healing Doctor: Repairs broken configs or corrupted binaries with a single keystroke.
  • Focus: Dropped Desktop/macOS/Windows support to hyper-focus on Android, Linux, and SBCs.

MinePath 💣

One chicken. One minefield. No second chances.

MinePath is a browser-based and mobile minesweeper survival game. Navigate a hidden minefield tile by tile, find the checkpoint, and get out before the timer runs out. Step on a mine — instant game over.

The Sanctuary Update (V4)

  • The Sanctuary Hub: A persistent hub you build and grow over time, earning passive seed income.
  • Skill Tree: Unlock 21 permanent passive abilities using Feather Points across four skill paths (Scout, Tank, Merchant, Skin-exclusive).
  • Endless Mode: A brand new auto-scrolling tile runner across 5 themed worlds (Classic, Lava, Ice, Jungle, Galaxy).
  • Hatchery & Eggs: Collect and incubate Brown, Blue, and Golden eggs to unlock pets and skins.
  • Player Leveling: Earn XP by surviving to level up and unlock new skills.
